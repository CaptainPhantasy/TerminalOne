# TerminalOne — precision prompt

The dedicated TerminalOne mark is a machined terminal monolith with an integrated T1, prompt chevron and baseline cursor. It is not the shared Floyd’s Labs prism.

Graphite, warm paper and restrained brass give the mark instrument-grade clarity without bloom or fluorescent accents. Every SVG has a transparent outer canvas; every PNG is RGBA and every ICO includes 16, 32 and 48 pixel variants. The letterforms and symbols are paths, with no font or network dependency.

Use the supplied icons at their native sizes. Leave the transparent perimeter intact; do not flatten onto a square background. The existing hero artwork remains a marketing illustration, not a product screenshot.
